Our Story

Built on Speed, Trust & Quality

The Car Tell was built on a simple idea — getting quality tyres and reliable auto care shouldn't be slow, expensive, or complicated. Based right here on Bram Fischer Drive in Randburg, our team is dedicated to keeping vehicles of every make and model safely on the road.

From fast tyre fitting to full vehicle repairs, we combine hands-on expertise with honest pricing so every customer drives away confident in their vehicle — and in the people who worked on it.

Whether you're popping in for a puncture repair or a full set of new tyres, you'll always get the same promise: Quality Service. Trusted Care.
